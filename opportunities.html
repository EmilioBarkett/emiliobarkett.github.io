What I'm Looking For

I'm open to full-time roles in technical AI governance and AI safety research. I want to keep doing the kind of work this site describes: empirical research on how AI systems behave and how organizations manage the risks of adopting them, grounded in social-science methods and aimed at problems that matter before they become disasters.

Specifically, I'm looking for:

  • Research management or fieldbuilding roles, building on what I've run at SPAR, CAIAC, and SIRA.
  • Research roles in AI governance, technical AI safety, or societal impacts, at a frontier lab, think tank, or policy organization.
  • Positions that combine social-science methods with technical AI safety questions, rather than treating them as separate tracks.

My CV and research go into the specifics. If something here sounds like a fit, or you just want to talk, get in touch.
